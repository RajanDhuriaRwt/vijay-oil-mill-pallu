Traditional Cattle Nutrition

Made Around Pure Binola Khal

Vijay Binola Khal is based on pure Kacchi Ghani Binola Khal and forms part of Vijay Oil Mill's cattle feed product range. It is intended for farmers looking for a traditional cottonseed-based feed ingredient for their animals.

According to the provided product information, it contains up to 24% protein and up to 20% oil. It also contains a higher amount of cellulose fibre that is described as easy to digest.

Its nutritional characteristics make Vijay Binola Khal a practical addition to a properly managed cattle feeding routine.
